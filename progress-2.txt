What to build (original spec):
Add velocity and deceleration physics to vehicle entities. Each vehicle spawns at the far
end of its arm and moves toward the stop line at max_speed. It decelerates when within
min_gap of the vehicle ahead or the stop line itself. All approaches hold red during this
slice to isolate the physics. New vehicles are suppressed when the per-approach queue
reaches 15 vehicles (models spillback).

Parameters per type (defaults, not user-configurable in this slice):
  MC:    max_speed=90, decel=180, min_gap=8
  CAR:   max_speed=70, decel=140, min_gap=12
  JEP:   max_speed=55, decel=110, min_gap=16
  BUS:   max_speed=45, decel=90,  min_gap=20
  TRUCK: max_speed=45, decel=90,  min_gap=20
(all in canvas-units/s; scaled by sc factor)

Spawn rate: 1 vehicle per approach every (3600 / volume_pcu_hr) simulated seconds,
sampled from queue_series_after average to determine arrival rate.

Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Vehicles accelerate to max_speed after spawning
- [ ] Vehicles decelerate smoothly when within min_gap of the vehicle ahead or stop line
- [ ] No vehicle overshoots the stop line or clips into the vehicle ahead
- [ ] Per-approach cap of 15 vehicles enforced; new spawns suppressed when queue is full
- [ ] Simulation plays correctly at 1×/5×/10× speed multipliers
- [ ] Reset clears all vehicle entities and resets sim clock

### Issue 10 — Signal phasing + before/after modes

What to build:
Wire signal control into vehicle movement. After mode uses Webster's per-chunk cycle_length
and green_splits from the existing timingApi response — vehicles on a green approach proceed
through the stop line and travel across the intersection box, then are removed. Before mode
uses unsignalized gap-acceptance: a vehicle at the stop line checks if the conflicting
approach gap is > 6s; if so, it proceeds. signal_off chunks (timing.signal_off === true)
use the Before/yield behavior regardless of toggle.

Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] After mode: signal cycles through approaches using Webster's splits; green approach
      vehicles clear the stop line, red approach vehicles hold
- [ ] Before mode: gap-acceptance logic — vehicle at stop line yields until 6s gap in
      conflicting flow, then proceeds
- [ ] signal_off chunks display amber signal dots and use gap-acceptance regardless of toggle
- [ ] Before/after toggle switches mode live without resetting sim clock
- [ ] Chunk selector change resets vehicle entities and sim clock (existing behavior preserved)
- [ ] Signal dot color: green=active green, red=red, amber=signal_off or before mode

Blocked by: Issue 9

### Issue 11 — Data-driven type mix from aggregation API

What to build:
Fetch /aggregation/history (existing endpoint, existing aggregationApi.history() service)
for the last complete hour per intersection. Derive the object_type distribution (MC%/JEP%/
CAR%/BUS%/TRUCK%) per street_id. When spawning a vehicle on a given approach, sample its
type stochastically from that distribution. Falls back to a citywide default mix
(MC=50%, CAR=30%, JEP=15%, BUS=3%, TRUCK=2%) if the aggregation fetch returns no rows for
that approach.

Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] SignalTiming page co-fetches aggregation history alongside simulation + timing data
- [ ] Type distribution computed per street_id from object_type counts
- [ ] Vehicle type on spawn is sampled from the per-approach distribution
- [ ] Fallback default mix applied when a street has no detection rows
- [ ] No new backend endpoints

Blocked by: Issue 8 (entity type must exist before type sampling can be wired in)

### Issue 12 — Turn path routing + intersection clearing

What to build:
After a vehicle crosses the stop line on green/yield, it follows a path to its exit arm.
Turn assignment uses a fixed split: 70% through, 15% left-turn, 15% right-turn (constant,
not user-configurable). Through vehicles travel straight across the center box to the
opposite arm. Left/right vehicles follow a simple two-segment path (straight to center,
then pivot to exit arm). Vehicles are removed from the entity list once they exit the arm
boundary, preventing center-box congestion.

Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Turn direction assigned at stop-line crossing (70/15/15 split, seeded per vehicle id)
- [ ] Through vehicles travel straight to exit arm and are removed at arm boundary
- [ ] Left/right vehicles follow a two-segment path through the center box and are removed
- [ ] Vehicle count in center box does not grow unbounded during extended play
- [ ] Per-approach cap (15 vehicles) counts only queuing vehicles, not vehicles in transit
- [ ] Animation remains smooth at 10× speed with vehicles in transit

Blocked by: Issue 10
